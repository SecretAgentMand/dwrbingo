Frequently Asked Questions

Some of these goals are impossible in this seed!

It's hard to avoid this in DWR since enemies can be inaccessible, certain gear can be unavailable, etc. Unless mcgrew or juef get interested in a "make sure everything is accessible for bingo" flag, hint hint, it's best to just accept and don't go any further than bingo to triple bingo/lockout.

Where are the NPCs to take Gwaelin to?

To reach the Dragonlord take that left turn at Albuquerque.

The Radish Vendor is the non-functional on the left side of Cantlin.

The dying soldier in Brecconary is in the locked inn room on the right.

The Puff Puff woman is next to the bath in the village of Kol.

The 'I hate people' woman in Garinham is the woman in the locked back building.

The jealous soldier in Tantegel is tucked into an alcove on the way to the MP restoring old wizard.

To reach the couple in Rimuldar, don't cross the bridge and go to the northeast and southwest corners.

How do you unequip the Fighter's Ring?

On the south side of Rimuldar behind the inn, a guard will say you are too young to be married and remove the Fighter's Ring.

Where do I remove curses in Brecconary?

An old wizard in a building in the central-right side of town.
